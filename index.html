About

Hi, I’m Chidinma.

I’m new to photography, and honestly, that’s part of the magic.

I started picking up a camera because I kept noticing things I didn’t want to forget: the way light falls on an ordinary street, the half-second before a friend bursts out laughing, a stranger’s face in a quiet moment.

I photograph places, people, and the friends who make my life feel full. I’m based in Ottawa, still learning every single day, and chasing the moments that matter, the small, soft ones most people walk past.

— Chidinma (@takenbyfave)
